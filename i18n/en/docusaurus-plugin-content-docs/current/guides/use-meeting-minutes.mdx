# Create and manage meeting minutes

Create minutes through a Chat Room's meeting feature. Recording and transcription process personal information; confirm organization retention policy and obtain every participant's consent before starting.

## Prepare and start recording

1. Open the Room's **Meeting notes** tool and start a new meeting. Agent-only Rooms or missing permissions can prevent starting; check the displayed explanation.
2. In preparation, choose whether to use interpretation and select the two languages. Optionally enter a topic, terms, background, and translation mappings. Save recurring recognition settings as a preset or select an existing preset.
3. Select the recording start action to open consent. Check the duration and recording/participant-notification agreements, then select **Agree and start**.
4. Check microphone connection and recording status. Opening preparation or saving settings does not itself start recording.

You can pause and resume while recording. Do not start transcription without the required consent or when recording is prohibited.

## Display mode and live translation sharing

Use the display action in preparation or the live meeting menu to show captions in a separate view. Choose the original/translation view and adjust its size.

A live translation link allows **anyone who knows the link to view captions**. Check participant consent and recipients before sharing. The link closes when the meeting ends; it is not permanent sharing of the saved meeting record.

## Review transcription and delivery

Read the transcript and organize paragraphs in the Meeting panel while the meeting runs. At the end, choose:

- <strong><span data-ui-label="chat.meeting.endDeliver">Deliver to Agent</span></strong>: save the transcript, generate a summary, and send transcript and summary artifacts to the Room.
- <strong><span data-ui-label="chat.meeting.endDiscard">Discard</span></strong>: soft-delete the minutes to exclude them from the archive.

The same choice appears when the time limit ends. An empty or unsaved transcript cannot produce a summary. Inspect the content before delivery and confirm the delivery or discard result afterward.

## Past minutes and summary versions

Open past minutes from the Room's meeting history. Finalized minutes show transcript and summary and support summary regeneration. Regeneration saves a new version; check the current version before sharing important decisions.

Download transcripts as `.txt` or `.md` from the history panel. Preserve required originals and summaries in approved storage before deletion. Deleting minutes records a reason and may make referenced results inaccessible from Chat.

Search meeting records and filter by period. Open a record to switch between summary and transcript; permitted users can edit its title or speaker information and delete it. Use text or Markdown downloads and preserve needed content before editing or deletion.

## Manual review after validation failure

If distortion or factual errors are detected, <strong><span data-ui-label="chat.meeting.validation.manualReviewRequired">Manual review is required.</span></strong> and <strong><span data-ui-label="chat.meeting.validation.reviewCta">Start Manual Review</span></strong> appear. Users who can see the action should:

1. Compare decisions, numbers, owners, and deadlines with the original transcript.
2. Select <strong><span data-ui-label="chat.meeting.validation.reviewCta">Start Manual Review</span></strong>.
3. Score M8, M10, M11, and M13 from 0–5, and S3 and S4 from 0–3.
4. Select <strong><span data-ui-label="chat.meeting.attestation.submit">Submit Review</span></strong> to rerun validation.

Scores do not edit the summary. If it is wrong, regenerate from the transcript or follow the correction procedure. Do not raise scores just to pass validation.

## Errors and privacy

For processing errors, inspect the message and meeting status instead of creating duplicate meetings. Recordings, transcripts, translations, and summaries may contain personal or customer data; limit recipients and retention when moving them through Chat, webhooks, or external tools.

Meeting-domain validation and summary-quota dashboards are separate operational concerns. Use manual review only when its action is actually shown.
