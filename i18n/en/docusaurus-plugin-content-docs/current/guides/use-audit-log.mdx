# Read and export Audit Log

Audit Log records important permission, Agent, and configuration changes. Read and export permissions may be restricted separately.

## Find records

1. Open **Audit Log**.
2. Filter by date, event type, Agent, and severity.
3. Open an entry to inspect before/after differences and related information.

Narrow time, organization, and Agent scope first. Broad exports may include other users' operational information.

## Export

When filtered results exist, choose CSV or JSON:

1. Apply necessary filters.
2. Recheck count and scope.
3. Select **CSV** or **JSON**.
4. Save to an approved location and record recipients and retention period.

Treat exports as access-controlled operational data. Do not upload them to Chat Rooms or public issues. Dispose of them according to retention policy after the investigation.

Check who performed which action, affected resources and organizations, sensitive before/after values, and whether follow-up requires access revocation, credential rotation, or Agent restart.

Audit Log assists investigation; it does not automatically roll back changes. Verify current settings and affected users/Agents before reversing anything.
