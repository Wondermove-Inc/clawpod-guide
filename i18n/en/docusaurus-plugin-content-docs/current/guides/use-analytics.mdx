# Review Agent operations with Analytics

Analytics shows activity, cost, and performance for accessible Agents and organizations. Metrics inform operations but cannot establish a conversation or incident's cause on their own.

The sidebar offers **Overview**, **Costs**, and **Monitoring**. Period selectors include ranges such as 24 hours, seven days, 30 days, and 90 days. Compare the same period and organization scope across views.

## Review sequence

1. Check request volume, conversations, errors, and anomalies in Overview.
2. Compare period, model, and usage changes with cost increases in Costs.
3. For Monitoring warnings, inspect System health, Agent logs, and related webhook Events together.

User usage, conversations, and costs are operational data. Access only what your role and task require. Check screenshots and exports for personal/customer data before sharing.

For poor performance or rising cost, inspect period, Agent, organization, and recent configuration changes before changing models, credentials, or permissions. Act in a small scope first.
