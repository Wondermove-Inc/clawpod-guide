# Manage tenant announcements

This guide is for tenant administrators who can see **Announcements**. It is not a task for ordinary members without announcement permissions.

## Compose an announcement

1. Select **Admin → Platform → Announcements**.
2. Choose **Create Announcement**.
3. Enter a title and Markdown content, within the displayed limits of 500 and 10,000 characters respectively.
4. Review start/end dates and whether it is active.
5. Optionally enter a link. The form accepts a link starting with `/portal/` or `https://`.
6. Review the tenant and organization targets. Choose all or specific organizations within your permitted tenant scope.
7. Review content, recipients, and dates before creating it. Use **Cancel** to leave without creating an announcement.

Check the saved result and the total, active, and scheduled counts in the list. An error is not a successful save. Whether a recipient sees the announcement also depends on the targeting and time conditions.

A page description referring to the whole platform does not extend a tenant administrator's reach into other tenants. Keep passwords, credentials, and personal data out of announcements.
