# Use Chat Archive

Chat Archive is a view of accessible Chat Room history, not a separate export store. The list follows `core:chat:List` permissions and tenant, organization, and internal-visibility rules.

## Find and read history

1. Open **Chat Archive** in the Portal.
2. Search by Room name to narrow the list.
3. Select a Room to open its message history.
4. Scroll up for older messages. New messages arrive through WebSocket; an immediate refresh interval during disconnection is not guaranteed.

Opening a Room updates its read status. Access only Rooms available in your list; do not guess or share other Room addresses or IDs.

## Create and delete Rooms

Use **+** at the top right to create a direct or group Room. Use **Select** to choose multiple existing Rooms for deletion.

Delete only Rooms you can manage and check the impact on other participants. Open and download required conversations' artifacts before deletion. Chat Archive does not export an entire conversation.

To send messages or change participants, follow the [Chat Room guide](./use-chat-room.mdx).
